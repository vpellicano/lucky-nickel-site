# Lucky Nickel SEO and answer-engine plan

Updated August 20, 2026.

## Position to own

Lucky Nickel should become the clearest food-first answer for people looking for kitchen-witch seasoning blends, cooking with intention, ingredient folklore, and practical magical seasoning. The brand story and Bex's firsthand experience are the moat. Do not publish generic, mass-produced folklore articles to chase traffic.

Primary query cluster:

- Lucky Nickel Company
- kitchen-witch seasoning blends
- cooking with intention
- seasoning blends with ingredient symbolism
- magical seasoning blends

Product query cluster:

- everyday seasoning salt
- salt-free Italian seasoning blend
- taco seasoning blend
- what to use seasoning salt on
- herbs and spices traditionally associated with love, protection, and intention

## Build decisions

- Put the specific category in the title, description, first paragraph, and visible product language.
- Answer four likely questions in short, indexable HTML instead of hiding them behind tabs or JavaScript.
- Establish the entity with Organization, WebSite, ItemList, and Product JSON-LD. Do not add prices, reviews, availability, nutrition claims, or certifications before they are true.
- Use Bex's original family story, a clear founder identity, and an explicit commercial-production answer as trust signals.
- Use high-resolution, relevant images with useful alt text, captions, and `max-image-preview:large` for Google Images and Discover eligibility.
- Keep the page fast and mobile-first: static HTML and CSS, responsive image sizes, lazy-loaded below-fold images, no framework, no tracking script.
- Publish `robots.txt`, a canonical URL, and an XML sitemap. Submit the site to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools after deployment; enable IndexNow when the content cadence begins.
- Add individual recipe and ingredient-story pages only after Bex supplies firsthand photos, tests, and original commentary. Those pages can use Recipe structured data when the visible recipe is complete.

## Content sequence

1. Founder story and first three blends.
2. One original page per blend with uses, sensory description, tested recipes, ingredient symbolism, and Bex's video.
3. Short, useful answers: what kitchen magic means to Bex, how to cook with intention, and how commercial seasoning blends are made.
4. Recipe posts tied to actual TikToks, with transcript, exact ingredients, original images, and concise author notes.
