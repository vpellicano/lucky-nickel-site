Before the company, there was a rule.

Do not feed your family your anger.

The short answers

What is Lucky Nickel?

What is a kitchen-witch seasoning blend?

A useful culinary blend paired with traditional ingredient symbolism and a small moment of intention while you cook.

Is flavor still the point?

Always. If it does not make dinner better, it does not belong in a Lucky Nickel jar.

Is it made in a home kitchen?

No. Every saleable jar will be commercially blended, filled, sealed, labeled, and lot-coded for local and interstate sales.

Can I order it yet?

Not yet. Bex is building the first three blends with a qualified commercial food co-manufacturer.
